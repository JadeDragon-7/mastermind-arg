OFFICIAL ULTIMATE RECORDS

OVERVIEW

The following document explains how to read the public Ultimate records provided by The Official Ultimate Website. The document will explain in detail what each section includes, as well as definitions for any terms used in the documents.

LAYOUT

All records will include the given first and last name of the applicant, as well as the current status of the applicant.

STATUS

There are several different statuses a record can have:

ACTIVE — The applicant is the current registered owner of the Ultimate.

FAILED — The applicant has failed all three attempts of the original testing or retesting of their chosen Ultimate.

SUSPENDED — The applicant's ownership of the Ultimate has expired, and they have not re-tested yet. There is no time limit for how long a record can remain on this category unless the Ultimate is reassigned.

PENDING — The applicant has passed their Ultimate test and reserved their Ultimate, but is waiting to turn of-age.

PAUSED — The applicant has started the testing process, but has not failed or passed their Ultimate test yet. There is no time limit for how long a record can remain on this category unless the Ultimate is reassigned.

ARCHIVED — The applicant no longer owns the Ultimate, and can not regain it. This can be for a number of reasons, from reassignment to fatality of the applicant. When a record is marked as Archived, there will be no more additions to the record.

CATEGORY

Category refers to the generation the applicant is in. The applicant will either be in generation X, Y, Z, or A, hyphened with the year the applicant was born.

REGION

Region depicts what location the applicant tested for their Ultimate in.

Countries with multiple regions have been colour-coded to make reading easier. For example, the United States of America is divided into four different regions, South, Midwest, West, and Northeast. For state or province divided countries, the state or province where the applicant tested will be given underneath.

Please note that if multiple regions have been listed, then the applicant tested in one region, moved permanently to another region, and retested there.

ATTEMPTS

Attempts documents the applicant's Ultimate process. Each attempt will be labeled with the date the test was taken, the age the applicant was when taking the test, their score, and whether the attempt resulted in a pass or a fail.

RETESTING

Similar to Attempts, Retesting shows the applicant's retesting process. Each attempt will be labeled with the date the test was taken, the age the applicant was when taking the test, their score, and whether the attempts resulted in a pass or a fail. Please note that each retest allows three attempts.

If the applicant has failed their test, this section will be marked as "Unable to retest."

HISTORY

History is a summary of the applicants Ultimate history by date. There can be several different categories, followed by the date the category was put into effect. If no history is listed, this means the applicant has not completed their Ultimate testing to these points.

PENDING — The date an applicant under the age of 15 passed their test. It marks the Ultimate as Reserved.

ACTIVE — The date the applicant passed their test or turned 15. It marks the moment the ownership of the Ultimate starts.

SUSPENDED — The date the Ultimate expires, typically 5 years from the date of activation.

PAUSED — The date the applicant passed their Ultimate test for an already taken Ultimate, and the period of time waiting for the response from the current owner.

ARCHIVED — The date that the records for the applicant are closed. The applicant can no longer test or retest.

OFFICIAL ULTIMATE

The official registered name of the Ultimate that the applicant applied for, currently owns, or attempted to apply for.

DATE OF EXPIRY

The date the ownership of the Ultimate expires, if the applicant currently owns the Ultimate. If the applicant does not own the Ultimate, there will be no date listed.
